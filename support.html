Support

Need help with Hasu, or have a suggestion? Write to guydaane@gmail.com and you will get a reply as soon as possible.

How does my plant grow?

Your plant grows once for every day you practice. Complete a session of any length, or end one early after at least a minute, and it grows. Extra sessions on the same day are added to your minutes but do not add growth.

What happens if I miss a day?

Nothing is lost. Your plant rests under an evening sky and your day streak starts again, but all growth stays. One session wakes the plant.

What happens at full bloom?

After thirty days of practice the plant moves to your pond, where it stays for good. A new seed then appears with a new practice: breath focus, body scan, open awareness, then loving-kindness.

Why is there no sound?

Check that "Bell sound" is switched on under More, that your phone is not in silent mode, and that the volume is up.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Progress is stored only on your device and is included in your phone's normal device backup. There is no account or cloud sync.

How does the subscription work?

Hasu is free to download. Starting a session requires a subscription: free for the first 3 days, then $39.99 per year (the price may differ by country). It renews automatically each year until you cancel.

How do I cancel?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Hasu. You can also open More in the app and tap "Manage subscription". Cancel at least a day before the trial or the year ends to avoid being charged. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

I subscribed but the app asks me to subscribe again

Open More and tap "Restore purchases". Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

How do I start over?

Open More and choose "Erase all progress". This cannot be undone. It does not affect your subscription.

Is Hasu a medical product?

No. Hasu is a tool for relaxation and for building a meditation habit. It does not diagnose or treat any condition.
