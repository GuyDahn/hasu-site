Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Hasu: Lotus Meditation ("the app") is published by Guy Dahan.

Summary

Hasu does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking, and your meditation data never leaves your device.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your meditation progress on your device only: for example your current plant, the number of sessions and minutes practiced, your streak, and your sound setting. This information never leaves your device and we have no access to it. Deleting the app, or choosing "Erase all progress" in the app, removes it.

Information we do not collect

  • No account, name, email address or contact details
  • No location, health or usage analytics
  • No advertising identifiers; the app contains no ads and no tracking
  • No third-party analytics or advertising software

Subscription purchases

Hasu is offered as a subscription bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and manages the subscription; we never receive your name, email address or payment details. The app only asks the App Store whether a subscription is active on your device. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

This website

This website is hosted by Vercel. It uses no cookies, analytics or tracking of its own. As with any website, the host may log technical information such as your IP address; see Vercel's privacy policy for details.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
