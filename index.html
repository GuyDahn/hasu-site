Meditation you can watch grow

Hasu is a small, quiet meditation app. Every day you practice, your plant grows a little: from a seed in the mud to a flower in full bloom. After thirty days it settles into your pond for good, and a new seed appears with a new practice to learn.

Growth you never lose

Your plant grows once for every day you practice, and it never shrinks. Miss a day and it simply rests under the evening sky. One short session wakes it again.

Four plants, four practices

Lotus for breath focus. Bamboo for the body scan. Bonsai for open awareness. A bodhi tree for loving-kindness. Each teaches one skill over thirty days.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Sessions work without a connection, and your progress stays on your phone.

Sit. Breathe. Bloom.

Three, five, ten or fifteen minutes. A breathing circle, a line of guidance, a soft bell.
