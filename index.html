The idea

Meditation you can watch grow.

Every day you practice, your plant grows a little: from a seed in the mud to a flower in full bloom. After thirty days it settles into your pond for good, and a new seed appears with a new practice to learn.

01

Growth you never lose

Your plant grows once for every day you practice, and it never shrinks. Miss a day and it simply rests under the evening sky. One short session wakes it again.

02

A new plant, a new skill

When one plant is fully grown, the next seed brings a different practice. Each takes thirty days of sitting, at your own pace, with a line of guidance to follow and a soft bell to close.

03

A pond that keeps what you grow

Every plant that reaches full bloom moves to your pond and stays there. It becomes a quiet record of the days you sat down and breathed.

Four plants, four practices

One skill at a time, thirty days each.

  1. 01

    Lotus

    Breath focus. Rest your attention on the breath and return to it, gently, each time it wanders.

  2. 02

    Bamboo

    Body scan. Move your attention slowly through the body and let each part soften.

  3. 03

    Bonsai

    Open awareness. Let sounds, thoughts and sensations come and go without following them.

  4. 04

    Bodhi tree

    Loving-kindness. Offer warm wishes to yourself, then to others, a little wider each time.
